How to play
Index triggers
Hold to shoot each web. Swinging starts when it hits. Release to let go.
Physical hand pull
With a web attached, pull back quickly to launch forward. Pull down to launch upward. Use both hands for more power.
People and webs
Aim at a person and hold a trigger. Move your web hand up, sideways, or toward you to move their body.
Thumbstick click (either hand)
Hold while close to a person to grab. Move your hand and release to throw. Punch by striking with your hand.
Side grips
Hold while attached to reel yourself toward the building.
A button
Hold on a rooftop or street to charge a super jump; release to leap.
Left stick / right stick
Move and steer / smooth turn.
B / X / Y
Return to starting roof / cycle pull power / pause and show controls.

Desktop: WASD move, mouse look, left/right mouse hold webs, Q/E reel, hold/release Space to jump, R reset, Esc pause.
