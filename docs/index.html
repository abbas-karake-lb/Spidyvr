How to play
Index triggers
Hold to shoot each web. Swinging starts when it hits. Release to let go.
Physical hand pull
With a web attached, pull back quickly to launch forward. Pull down to launch upward. Use both hands for more power.
People and webs
Aim at a person and hold a trigger. Move your web hand up, sideways, or toward you to move their body.
Thumbstick click (either hand)
Hold while close to a person to grab. Move your hand and release to throw. Punch by striking with your hand.
Side grips
Press near a floating gun, then keep holding to carry it. Trigger fires; release grip to drop or throw. Without a gun, hold while attached to reel your web.
A button
Hold on a rooftop or street to charge a super jump; release to leap.
Left stick / right stick
Move and steer / smooth turn.
B / X / Y
Return to starting roof / cycle pull power / pause and show controls.

Desktop: WASD move, mouse look, left/right mouse hold webs, Q/E hold gun nearby or reel, hold/release Space to jump, R reset, Esc pause.

Quarantine building · zombie challenge

Find the gold-crowned QUARANTINE tower on the eastern waterfront, beyond the city’s last row of buildings. Enter through the west-facing street doors. Two supply guns wait beside the entrance: hold a side grip to pick one up; use the trigger to fire. The teal Rapid fires automatically while held.

Clear all 40 infected across ten furnished floors, then reach the gold reward pedestal on the roof. Take the stairs, or enter the lift on its left side to go up and right side to go down; step out between rides. Your wrist shows health and remaining enemies. Four attacks defeat you and respawn you at the entrance. After clearing, leave the building: the encounter resets after ten minutes. The rooftop Warden is a powerful gold automatic weapon.
